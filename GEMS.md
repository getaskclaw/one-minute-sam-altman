## On ambition — what to build

> "Successful people create companies. More successful people create countries. The most successful people create religions."

— [Successful people](https://blog.samaltman.com/successful-people), 2013 *(quoting Qi Lu)*

> "The most successful founders do not set out to create companies. They are on a mission to create something closer to a religion, and at some point it turns out that forming a company is the easiest way to do so."

— [Successful people](https://blog.samaltman.com/successful-people), 2013

> "It is easier for a team to do a hard thing that really matters than to do an easy thing that doesn't really matter; audacious ideas motivate people."

— [What I Wish Someone Had Told Me](https://blog.samaltman.com/what-i-wish-someone-had-told-me), 2023

> "The most counterintuitive secret about startups is that it's often easier to succeed with a hard startup than an easy one."

— [Hard startups](https://blog.samaltman.com/hard-startups), 2020

> "You should start with an idea, not a company... The best way to start a company is to build interesting projects."

— [Startup advice, briefly](https://blog.samaltman.com/startup-advice-briefly), 2015

> "The best startup ideas are the ones that seem like bad ideas but are good ideas."

— [Startup advice, briefly](https://blog.samaltman.com/startup-advice-briefly), 2015

> "The best companies start out with ideas that don't sound very good."

— [Projects and companies](https://blog.samaltman.com/projects-and-companies), 2014

> "Great companies often look like bad ideas at the beginning—at a minimum, if it looks great, the seed round is likely to be overpriced."

— [Black swan seed rounds](https://blog.samaltman.com/black-swan-seed-rounds), 2014

> "The best ideas are fragile; most people don't even start talking about them at all because they sound silly."

— [Idea Generation](https://blog.samaltman.com/idea-generation), 2020

> "When you can say 'I am sure this is going to happen, I'm just not sure if we'll be the ones to do it', that's a good sign."

— [Idea Generation](https://blog.samaltman.com/idea-generation), 2020

> "I've come to believe that founder/company fit is as important as product/market fit."

— [Idea Generation](https://blog.samaltman.com/idea-generation), 2020

> "You are far better off starting a company that people laugh at but keeps growing relentlessly than a company with a beautiful office that seems serious but is always two quarters away from starting its growth ramp."

— [Super successful companies](https://blog.samaltman.com/super-successful-companies), 2015

> "If you can't figure out how to raise hundreds of millions of dollars, go work for SpaceX (joining a great company is a much better plan than starting a mediocre one)."

— [Value is created by doing](https://blog.samaltman.com/value-is-created-by-doing), 2014

## On work & productivity

> "It doesn't matter how fast you move if it's in a worthless direction. Picking the right thing to work on is the most important element of productivity and usually almost ignored."

— [Productivity](https://blog.samaltman.com/productivity), 2018

> "The right goal is to allocate your year optimally, not your day."

— [Productivity](https://blog.samaltman.com/productivity), 2018

> "You can get to about the 90th percentile in your field by working either smart or hard... but getting to the 99th percentile requires both."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "Extreme people get extreme results."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "It is much more important to work on the right thing than it is to work many hours. Most people waste most of their time on stuff that doesn't matter."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "Work really hard. Everyone wants a secret to success other than this; if it exists, I haven't found it yet. Keep doing this for 10 years."

— [Startup advice, briefly](https://blog.samaltman.com/startup-advice-briefly), 2015

> "Every day, figure out what the 2 or 3 most important things for you to do are. Do those and ignore other distractions."

— [Startup advice, briefly](https://blog.samaltman.com/startup-advice-briefly), 2015

> "I have yet to meet a slow-moving person who is very successful."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "You have to have great execution—far more people have good ideas than are willing to roll up their sleeves and get shit done."

— [Startup advice](https://blog.samaltman.com/startup-advice), 2013

> "Be a doer, not a talker."

— [The days are long but the decades are short](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-days-are-long-but-the-decades-are-short), 2015

> "Fast iteration can make up for a lot; it's usually ok to be wrong if you iterate quickly."

— [What I Wish Someone Had Told Me](https://blog.samaltman.com/what-i-wish-someone-had-told-me), 2023

## On people & networks

> "It really is true that you become an average of the people you spend the most time with."

— [The days are long but the decades are short](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-days-are-long-but-the-decades-are-short), 2015

> "I try to always ask myself when I meet someone new 'is this person a force of nature?' It's a pretty good heuristic for finding people who are likely to accomplish great things."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "Be overly generous with sharing the upside; it will come back to you 10x."

— [How To Be Successful](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-be-successful), 2019

> "Great people attract other great people; as soon as you get a mediocre person in the building, this entire phenomenon can unwind."

— [How to hire](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-hire), 2013

> "Stay away from people who are world-weary and belittle your ambitions. Unfortunately, this is most of the world. But they hold on to the past, and you want to live in the future."

— [Idea Generation](https://blog.samaltman.com/idea-generation), 2020

> "You want to be around people who don't make you feel stupid for mentioning a bad idea."

— [Idea Generation](https://blog.samaltman.com/idea-generation), 2020

> "Spend more time recruiting. Take risks on high-potential people with a fast rate of improvement. Look for evidence of getting stuff done in addition to intelligence."

— [What I Wish Someone Had Told Me](https://blog.samaltman.com/what-i-wish-someone-had-told-me), 2023

> "If you meet someone three times in three months, and notice detectable improvement each time, pay attention to that."

— [How to invest in startups](https://blog.samaltman.com/how-to-invest-in-startups), 2015

> "If you believe that intelligence and determination are evenly distributed, less than 5% of the best founders are born in the US."

— [The founder visa, again](https://blog.samaltman.com/the-founder-visa-again), 2016
